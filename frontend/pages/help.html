Frequently Asked Questions

How do I update my profile information?▼
Go to Settings → Profile tab. You can update your name, phone, date of birth, gender, blood type, height, and emergency contact details. Click "Save Profile" when done.
How do I change my password?▼
Navigate to Settings → Privacy & Security tab. Enter your current password and your new password (minimum 6 characters), then click "Update Password".
How do I log my daily health data?▼
From the Dashboard, click the "+ Add Health Data" button. Enter your heart rate, blood pressure, weight, temperature, and any notes. The data will be saved and analyzed by our AI.
What do the vital sign thresholds mean?▼
Thresholds define what's "normal" for you. In Settings → Health Preferences, you can customize the min/max ranges for heart rate, blood pressure, and temperature. Values outside these ranges will trigger warnings.
How do I book an appointment?▼
Go to the Appointments page from the sidebar. Click "Book Appointment", fill in the doctor name, specialization, date, and reason. You'll receive a confirmation and reminders.
Can I cancel an appointment?▼
Yes! Go to the Appointments page, find your appointment in the list, and click the "Cancel" button. Cancelled appointments will be marked accordingly.
Is my health data secure?▼
Absolutely. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), JWT authentication, and secure password hashing (bcrypt). Your data is stored locally in an encrypted database and is never shared with third parties.
How do I export my health data?▼
You have two options: 1) From the Dashboard, click "Export Report" for a CSV of recent data. 2) From Settings → Privacy, click "Download Your Data" for a complete JSON export of all your information.
What AI features are available?▼
Our AI provides: Health Insights (personalized recommendations based on your vitals), Diet Plans (nutrition suggestions), Exercise Recommendations, and an AI Chatbot for health-related questions.

Health Education Resources

Nutrition

Understanding Balanced Diet

Learn about macronutrients, micronutrients, and how to create a balanced meal plan for optimal health.

Key Tips:
  • Fill half your plate with vegetables
  • Choose whole grains over refined
  • Include lean protein in every meal
  • Stay hydrated — 8 glasses of water daily
Fitness

Exercise for Better Health

Regular physical activity reduces the risk of chronic diseases and improves mental health.

Recommended:
  • 150 minutes of moderate exercise/week
  • Strength training 2-3 times/week
  • Stretch before and after exercise
  • Start slow and gradually increase intensity
Mental Health

Managing Stress & Anxiety

Mental health is just as important as physical health. Learn techniques for stress management.

Practices:
  • Practice mindfulness meditation daily
  • Maintain a regular sleep schedule
  • Connect with friends and family
  • Limit screen time before bed
Heart Health

Understanding Your Vitals

Know what your numbers mean and when to seek medical attention.

Normal Ranges:
  • Heart Rate: 60-100 bpm (resting)
  • Blood Pressure: <120/80 mmHg
  • Temperature: 36.1-37.2°C
  • BMI: 18.5-24.9 (normal weight)
Sleep Health

Quality Sleep Guide

Sleep is essential for recovery, cognitive function and overall well-being.

Sleep Hygiene:
  • Aim for 7-9 hours per night
  • Keep a consistent sleep schedule
  • Avoid caffeine 6 hours before bed
  • Create a dark, cool sleeping environment
Prevention

Preventive Healthcare

Regular check-ups and screenings can catch health issues early.

Regular Check-ups:
  • Annual physical exam
  • Blood work every 6-12 months
  • Dental check-up every 6 months
  • Eye exam annually
